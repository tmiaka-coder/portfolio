Project Overview

NZ Trace & Memory is a Progressive Web App (PWA) built for two specific users — my partner and me — to document our road trip across New Zealand. The app lets us record GPS routes in real time, manually draw routes on the map (which are then snapped to actual roads via the Overpass API), and drop memory pins with photos and text at meaningful locations.

All data is stored in Firebase Firestore and Storage, with Google Authentication restricting access to our two accounts only. The app is fully installable on both iOS and Android as a PWA.

02

Background & Purpose

Ahead of our move to New Zealand in August 2026, I wanted to build something we would actually use every day on the road — not just a portfolio piece. The idea was simple: record where we go, how far we travel, and capture the moments that matter, all in one place.

It also became an opportunity to deepen my skills with React, real-time databases, geolocation APIs, and mobile-first PWA design in a real, production context.

03

Key Focus: Security & Privacy by Design

Two-User Access Control

  • Google Authentication: Only two specific Google accounts are permitted. Any other account is immediately rejected after sign-in — even if the URL is known.
  • Firestore allowlist: An allowedUsers collection in Firestore stores the two permitted UIDs. All security rules are enforced server-side, ensuring no data can be read or written by unauthorised users regardless of client-side code.
  • Environment variables: All Firebase credentials are stored in .env.local and excluded from version control via .gitignore, ensuring no secrets are exposed in the public repository.

Firestore & Storage Security Rules

  • All read/write operations require authentication and membership in the allowlist. Data validation (field types, string length limits) is enforced at the rule level, not just the client.
  • Storage uploads are limited to image files under 2MB, with write access restricted to the owning user's UID path.

05

Design Philosophy & Key Features

Accessibility-First

All interactive elements include appropriate ARIA labels and roles. Progress bars use role="progressbar" with aria-valuenow for screen reader compatibility. The safety modal on first load captures focus automatically, and all navigation is keyboard-accessible.

Mobile-First PWA

Designed as a smartphone-first experience with a fixed bottom navigation bar, touch-optimised controls, and full PWA support — installable on the home screen of both iOS and Android devices without an app store.

Personalisation

Each user can customise their pin colour (9 options), choose from three themed colour schemes (NZ Nature, Ocean, ALL Blacks), and set a personal avatar from preset icons or a custom uploaded photo.

Legal & Safety Compliance

A mandatory safety modal on first load warns against operating the app while driving and includes a liability disclaimer. GPS location data is only accessed after explicit user interaction, with purpose clearly communicated in the UI.
