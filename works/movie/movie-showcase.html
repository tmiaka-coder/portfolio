Video Production

Yakitori Shop Promo Video

An energetic short-form video designed to maximize engagement and drive foot traffic to a yakitori restaurant. Optimized for SNS with dynamic motion and strong sensory appeal.

Category Short Form Video
Platform Instagram / TikTok
Duration 4h
Tool Premiere Pro
01

Project Overview

This short promotional video was created for a yakitori restaurant to convey atmosphere, taste, and movement in a compact 15-second format. The goal was to make viewers feel the sizzle and excitement of dining in while scrolling through social media.

02

Storytelling Strategy

Three-act structure for completion rate

  • Opening (0–3s): Capture attention with close-ups of flaming skewers and sizzling meat.
  • Middle (4–10s): Show the chef's skilled hand movements and the rich texture of ingredients to build trust.
  • Closing (11–15s): Transition to smiling customers and a call to action encouraging reservations or follows.
03

Creative Focus

Visual impact through motion and texture

The edit emphasizes sizzling sound, steam, and close-up textures to create a sensory-rich experience. The pacing is designed to feel fast yet polished, matching modern SNS viewing habits.

04

Tech Stack

Editing

Adobe Premiere Pro

Assets

High-quality food photography and motion graphics

Delivery

SNS-ready vertical video

Performance

Optimized for mobile viewing and fast engagement

05

Approach

Modern SNS optimization

The video was designed to be immediately understandable without sound, with bold visuals and quick story beats suitable for Instagram Reels and TikTok.

Brand-focused visual tone

The color palette and visual rhythm were chosen to convey both the warmth of the restaurant and the premium quality of the food.
